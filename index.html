Calculation guide

Enter distinct work packages at a common reporting date.

BAC · Budget at completion
Sum of estimated total budgets.
PV · Planned value
Budget × planned completion / 100.
EV · Earned value
Budget × actual completion / 100.
AC · Actual cost
Actual spending to date.
CV · Cost variance
EV − AC. Positive = under cost; negative = over cost.
CV % · Cost variance percentage
(EV − AC) / EV × 100. Undefined when EV = 0.
SV · Schedule variance
EV − PV. Positive = ahead; negative = behind.
CPI / SPI
CPI = EV / AC. SPI = EV / PV. Values below 1 indicate lower efficiency.
EAC · Estimate at completion
BAC / CPI, assuming current cost efficiency continues.
ETC / VAC
ETC = EAC − AC. VAC = BAC − EAC.
Budget variance
BAC − AC; percentage = (BAC − AC) / BAC × 100. This comparison does not account for unfinished work.

Charts accumulate work packages in table order, not over time. Undefined ratios display “—”. AI uses an Isolation Forest with 80 trees and a score threshold of 0.60; at least 5 work packages are needed.
